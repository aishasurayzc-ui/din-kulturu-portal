📌 Prophet Muhammad, the Servant of Allah (2/2)

  • While the idolaters expected him to be an angel, the Qur'an corrected this mistaken idea by saying: "Say: I am only a human being like you" (Surah Fussilat, 6).
  • In the Declaration of Faith (Shahada), the phrase "the servant of Allah" comes before "His Messenger," and this order highlights that same point.

❌ COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS

❌ Some believe Prophet Muhammad was only a religious leader, not an ordinary human being.

✅ According to the Qur'an, Prophet Muhammad was a human being just like us; the difference was that revelation came to him — this makes him an even more attainable role model for us.

📌 Prophet Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah (1/3)

  • Messenger/Rasul/Nabi: a prophet who calls people to the true religion and receives revelation.
  • Risalah = the duty of being a messenger, Nubuwwah = the office of prophethood, Tabligh = conveying the revelation in full.
  • Prophet Muhammad became a prophet at age 40; he is Khatam al-Anbiya (the Seal of the Prophets) — no prophet will come after him (Surah al-Ahzab, 40).
  • He is a Prophet of Mercy (gentleness, compassion, tenderness): "We have sent you as nothing but a mercy to the worlds" (Surah al-Anbiya, 107).

🌍 A REAL-LIFE EXAMPLE

Even though he was stoned in Ta'if, Prophet Muhammad did not curse the people but forgave them instead — a concrete act that can serve as an example for conflict resolution today.

📌 Prophet Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah (2/3)

  • Although he was stoned in Ta'if, he did not curse its people.
  • He explains the Qur'an: he explained the prayer, the pilgrimage, the fast (the black and white threads = night and day), and "zulm" (=shirk).
  • He is a Warner (nadhir in the Qur'an) and a Bearer of Good News (bashir in the Qur'an).
  • He was sent to complete good character: "And indeed, you are of a great moral character" (Surah al-Qalam, 4).

💭 THINK AND ANSWER

📌 Prophet Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah (3/3)

  • When prophethood came to him, Khadijah comforted him because of his good character.
  • The angel of revelation, Gabriel, brought the first revelation in the Cave of Hira. He had the Prophet's Mosque built in Medina.
  • The Prophet's words, deeds, and approvals are the Sunnah.

📖 Unit 4 Topic Summary: Prophet Muhammad, Servant and Messenger of Allah

1️⃣ Prophet Muhammad, the Servant of Allah

The Prophet has two aspects: his human aspect and his prophetic aspect. He was created as a servant just like us, and fulfilled his duty of servitude throughout his life. He was born of a mother and father, engaged in trade, started a family, and joined the Hilf al-Fudul (Society of the Virtuous), which protected the rights of the poor in Mecca. While the idolaters expected him to be an angel, the Qur'an corrected this mistaken idea by saying "Say: I am only a human being like you" (Surah Fussilat, 6). In the Declaration of Faith (Shahada), the phrase "the servant of Allah" comes before "His Messenger," and this order highlights that same point.

🤔 Did You Know?

Prophet Muhammad is called the "Prophet of Mercy" because of the verse in the Qur'an: "We have sent you as nothing but a mercy to the worlds."

❌ Common Misconceptions

❌ Some believe Prophet Muhammad was only a religious leader, not an ordinary human being.

✅ According to the Qur'an, Prophet Muhammad was a human being just like us; the difference was that revelation came to him — this makes him an even more attainable role model for us.

2️⃣ Prophet Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah

Messenger/Rasul/Nabi: a prophet who calls people to the true religion and receives revelation. Risalah = the duty of being a messenger, Nubuwwah = the office of prophethood, Tabligh = conveying the revelation in full. Prophet Muhammad became a prophet at age 40; he is Khatam al-Anbiya (the Seal of the Prophets) — no prophet will come after him (Surah al-Ahzab, 40). He is a Prophet of Mercy (gentleness, compassion, tenderness): "We have sent you as nothing but a mercy to the worlds" (Surah al-Anbiya, 107). Although he was stoned in Ta'if, he did not curse its people.

He explains the Qur'an: he explained the prayer, the pilgrimage, the fast (the black and white threads = night and day), and "zulm" (=shirk). He is a Warner (nadhir in the Qur'an) and a Bearer of Good News (bashir in the Qur'an). He was sent to complete good character: "And indeed, you are of a great moral character" (Surah al-Qalam, 4). Khadijah comforted him because of his good character when prophethood came to him. The angel of revelation, Gabriel, brought the first revelation in the cave of Hira. He had the Prophet's Mosque built in Medina. The Prophet's words, deeds, and approvals are the Sunnah.
