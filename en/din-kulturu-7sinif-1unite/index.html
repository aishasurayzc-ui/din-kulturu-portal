  • In the realm of the unseen there are jinn (beings with free will, created from fire), Satan (a being expelled for refusing to prostrate before Adam), and angels.

📌 Seen and Unseen Beings (2/2)

  • Superstition and false belief are fabricated claims, contrary to reason, put forward in the name of religion.
  • Magic/sorcery is deceiving people through trickery; fortune-telling is drawing meaning from objects in order to learn the future.
  • These generally stem from ignorance and a lack of religious knowledge, and they cause personal and social harm.
