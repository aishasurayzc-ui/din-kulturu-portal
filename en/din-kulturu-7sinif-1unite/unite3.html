📖 Unit 3 Topic Summary: Moral Conduct

1️⃣ Good Moral Attitudes and Behaviors

Morality (Ahlak) refers to the good behaviors gained by putting into practice, through a person's innate qualities, rules aimed at goodness and happiness. Value (Değer) is a superior quality or worth; attitude (Tutum) is the course followed when facing a situation; behavior (Davranış) consists of externally observable actions. The two fundamental sources of moral principles are the Holy Qur'an and the Sunnah of the Prophet.

Good moral values include: Justice (Adalet — truthfulness, equality, giving everyone their due), Friendship (Dostluk — a bond formed with people who are loved and trusted), Honesty/Truthfulness (Dürüstlük/Doğruluk, or Sıdk — speaking the truth), Self-control (Öz denetim / otokontrol — regulating one's behavior), Patience (Sabır — enduring hardship without losing one's composure), Respect (Saygı — treating what is sacred or one's elders with care), Love (Sevgi — a feeling of close interest and attachment), Responsibility (Sorumluluk — owning the consequences of one's behavior), Patriotism (Vatanseverlik — loving and protecting one's homeland — the concepts of Homeland, Martyr, and Veteran), Helpfulness (Yardımseverlik — supporting those in need through charity [İnfak] and generosity).

🤔 Did You Know?

Prophet Salih (peace be upon him) was sent to the people of Thamud and was supported by the miracle of a she-camel that emerged from a rock.

❌ Common Misconceptions

❌ Some people think that self-control (otokontrol) is an innate trait that cannot be developed.

✅ Self-control is a skill that can be developed through effort — the ability to manage one's momentary impulses in order to reach an important goal.

2️⃣ Learning About a Prophet: Prophet Salih (peace be upon him)

Prophet Salih, whose father was Ubayd, was the prophet sent to the people of Thamud. The people of Thamud lived in the Hijr region after the people of Ad and were known for carving dwellings out of stone. As the tribe gradually turned to idol worship, Prophet Salih preached the oneness of Allah (tawhid) to them. Allah sent a she-camel emerging from a rock as a miracle; when the tribe slaughtered the camel, they were destroyed three days later by a terrifying, thunderous sound. This narrative (kıssa) is related so that lessons may be drawn from it about not rebelling against Allah.

🌍 A Real-Life Example

A student putting off the urge to play before an exam in order to study is a concrete, everyday example of self-control.

3️⃣ Learning a Surah: Surah al-Falaq

Surah al-Falaq is a surah of the Qur'an revealed in the Medinan period, consisting of 5 verses. "Falaq" means the light of daybreak. It teaches seeking refuge in Allah from the evil of what He has created, the darkness of night, sorcerers, and the envious. Together with Surah an-Nas, it is known as "al-Muawwidhatayn" (the two protectors). The Prophet recited this surah before sleeping and after prayers.

TransliterationMeaning
Kul eûzü birabbil felakSay, "I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak,
Min şerri mâ halakFrom the evil of that which He created,
Ve min şerri ğâsikin izâ vekabAnd from the evil of darkness when it settles,
Ve min şerrin neffâsâti fil ukadAnd from the evil of the blowers in knots,
Ve min şerri hâsidin izâ hasedAnd from the evil of an envier when he envies."
