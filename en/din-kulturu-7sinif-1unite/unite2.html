📌 The Practice of Hajj in Islam and Its Importance (1/2)

  • Hajj is an obligatory (fard) act of worship performed by Muslims who have the means, during the month of Dhu al-Hijjah, by entering the state of ihram, standing at Arafat (wuquf), and circling the Kaaba (tawaf).
  • It is reported that Hajj began in the time of Prophet Adam, and that Prophet Abraham and his son Prophet Ishmael rebuilt the Kaaba.
  • Hajj was made obligatory in the 9th year of the Hijra and is one of the five pillars of Islam.

📌 The Practice of Hajj in Islam and Its Importance (2/2)

  • Hajj has three obligatory acts (fard): entering the state of ihram, standing at Arafat (wuquf), and circling the Kaaba (tawaf).

🤔 DID YOU KNOW?

Zamzam water is a miracle from Allah that appeared while Hazrat Hagar was searching for water for her son, Prophet Ishmael.

📌 How Hajj Is Performed (1/2)

  • Ihram is entered at the miqat, and the talbiyah prayer is recited. Going around the Kaaba once is called a shawt; going around it seven times is called tawaf.
  • Sa'i is the walk performed between Safa and Marwah. Wuquf is standing at Arafat. Zamzam is the sacred water offered to Hazrat Hagar.
  • The Black Stone (Hajar al-Aswad) is the black stone set in the corner of the Kaaba.

❌ COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS

❌ Some people think that the practice of sacrifice consists merely of slaughtering an animal.

✅ According to the Qur'an, neither the meat nor the blood of the sacrifice reaches Allah; what reaches Him is the believer's piety (taqwa) — sacrifice embodies an awareness of sharing and self-sacrifice.

📌 How Hajj Is Performed (2/2)

  • Places: the Kaaba (the first house of worship on earth, Baytullah), the hills of Safa and Marwah, Muzdalifah (between Arafat and Mina, the night stay), Mina (where the sacrifice is slaughtered and the devil is stoned), Arafat (the site of the standing on the Day of Arafah).

🌍 A REAL-LIFE EXAMPLE

Dividing the sacrificial meat into three parts (for the household, those in need, and neighbors/guests) and sharing it is a concrete example of solidarity.

📌 Umrah and Its Importance

  • Umrah is an act of worship performed outside the Hajj season, in which a person in ihram circles the Kaaba, performs sa'i between Safa and Marwah, and then shaves or trims their hair to exit the state of ihram.
  • Hajj is obligatory (fard); Umrah is sunnah. Hajj is performed once a year, while Umrah can be performed at any time. There is no standing (wuquf), stoning of the devil, or sacrifice in Umrah.

💭 THINK AND ANSWER

How does the fact that millions of Muslims circle the Kaaba at the same time, wearing the same garment (ihram), reflect Islam's understanding of equality?

📌 The Practice of Sacrifice, Prophet Ishmael, Al-An'am 162 (1/3)

  • Sacrifice is the slaughtering of certain animals on specific days with the intention of worship; it is a wajib (obligatory) act of worship. It is offered during the first 3 days of Eid al-Adha.
  • A sheep or goat may be sacrificed on behalf of 1 person; cattle on behalf of 1-7 people; a camel (over 5 years old) on behalf of 1-7 people.
  • The sacrificial meat is divided into three: for the household, for those in need, and for neighbors/guests.

👨‍👩‍👧 DOING THIS WITH MY FAMILY

Plan with your family to offer meat to your neighbors or to those in need during Eid al-Adha.

📌 The Practice of Sacrifice, Prophet Ishmael, Al-An'am 162 (2/3)

  • A votive offering (adak/nadhr) is a sacrifice slaughtered upon the fulfillment of a wish; an aqiqah is slaughtered in gratitude for the birth of a child.
  • In Alevism-Bektashism, the slaughtering of a sacrifice is called tığlama.
  • Prophet Ishmael is the son of Prophet Abraham and Hazrat Hagar; the practice of sacrifice began when he was to be offered as a sacrifice (Surah as-Saffat).
