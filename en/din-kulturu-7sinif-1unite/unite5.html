📌 Reasons for Differences in Interpretation in Understanding Religion

  • Understanding of religion is the way people perceive, interpret, and apply God's commands.
  • Religion itself does not change (the Qur'an and the Sunnah), but the understanding of religion can vary according to the person, society, and period.
