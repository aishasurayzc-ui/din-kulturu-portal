📙 Unit 3 Topic Summary: Religion is Good Character

People who are careful and conscientious about following the commands and prohibitions of religion and the principles it sets out are described as religious/pious. A pious person must first sincerely embrace the principles of faith and carefully perform acts of worship such as prayer, fasting, pilgrimage, and almsgiving. However, this is not enough: a pious person must also have good character. Religion asks people not only to have faith and worship, but also to follow the principles of good character.

1️⃣ Religion is Good Character

In Islam, besides the principles of faith and worship, the principles of good character also hold an important place. The Qur'an states the qualities a good person should have: being truthful and honest, helping the poor, being patient, working honestly, and treating all living things with compassion and mercy.

Verse: "...Righteousness is not that you turn your faces toward the east or the west, but righteous is the one who believes in Allah, the Last Day, the angels, the Book, and the prophets, and gives wealth, in spite of love for it, to relatives, orphans, the needy... and establishes prayer and gives zakah... and [those who are] patient in poverty and hardship and during battle. Those are the ones who have been true, and it is those who are the righteous." (Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 177.)

Religion asks that moral principles be followed and commands good behavior: being truthful, acting honestly, helping those in difficulty, looking after the poor, respecting parents and elders, maintaining ties with relatives, doing good to neighbors.

Religion also forbids bad behavior that harms the individual and society: lying, slander, deceiving others, being unjust to people, polluting the environment, mistreating animals.

Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) — The Finest Example of Good Character
Verse 4 of Surah Al-Qalam states that Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) was of exalted character. Surah Al-Ahzab also states: "Indeed in the Messenger of Allah you have a good example for whoever hopes in Allah and the Last Day and remembers Allah often." (Surah Al-Ahzab, verse 21.)

Our Prophet (peace be upon him) always spoke the truth, safeguarded what was entrusted to him, and kept his promises. He was extremely merciful toward people; kind-spoken, cheerful, and gentle-natured. He was careful to avoid vices such as arrogance, jealousy, lying, gossip, and slander. He stood against injustice and always sided with the one in the right.

Hadith: "I was sent only to perfect good character." (Muwatta, Husn al-Khulq, 8.) — "A Muslim is the one from whose tongue and hand people are safe." (An-Nasa'i, Faith, 8.)

🤔 Did You Know?

Through Surah Al-Fatihah, recited in the five daily prayers, our Prophet asks Allah forty times a day to be guided to the straight path.

❌ Common Misconceptions

❌ Some people think good character is limited to acts of worship such as praying and fasting.

✅ Good character also covers daily behavior such as honesty, respect, love, and neighborly rights — our Prophet said, "I was sent only to perfect good character."

2️⃣ The Foundation of Human Relationships: Love and Respect

Love is one of the most basic feelings that bind people together. Through love, good relationships and friendships are built among people. Respect means knowing that everyone has certain rights and being careful to honor them. Love and respect are more meaningful when they exist together.

👨‍👩‍👧 Love and Respect in Relationships with Parents
Our mother and father make many sacrifices for us throughout their lives. In Islam, the rights of parents hold great importance.

Verse: "And We have enjoined upon man goodness to parents..." (Surah Al-Ahqaf, verse 15.) — "Your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and that you be kind to parents..." (Surah Al-Isra, verses 23-24.)

Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) said: "The greatest of major sins are associating partners with Allah, and disobeying and hurting one's parents." (Bukhari, Al-Adab, 6.) — "Parents are among the gates that lead a person into Paradise." (Tirmidhi, Al-Birr, 3.)

👫 Love and Respect Among Siblings
Our siblings are the people closest to us. Our Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "He who does not show mercy to our young and respect to our elders is not one of us." (Tirmidhi, Al-Birr, 15.)

👪 Love and Respect Among Relatives
Maintaining good relations with relatives is called silah al-rahim (maintaining family ties). Visiting relatives, asking after their well-being, exchanging gifts, and sharing joy and sorrow are all requirements of maintaining family ties.

Verse: "...And fear Allah, through whom you ask one another, and the wombs [i.e., family ties]. Indeed Allah is ever, over you, an Observer." (Surah An-Nisa, verse 1.)

Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) said: "Whoever severs ties with relatives will not enter Paradise." (Bukhari, Al-Adab, 11.) — "Whoever wishes for their provision to be increased should maintain ties with their relatives!" (Bukhari, Al-Adab, 12.)

🏘️ Love and Respect Among Neighbors
Neighbors are people whose homes are adjoining or close to one another. The proverb "A neighbor needs even the ashes of their neighbor" describes this closeness.

Verse: "Worship Allah and associate nothing with Him, and to parents do good, and to relatives, orphans, the needy, the near neighbor, the neighbor farther away... [do good]." (Surah An-Nisa, verse 36.)

Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) said: "One whose neighbor is not safe from their harm will not enter Paradise." (Muslim, Faith, 73.) — "Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should not harm their neighbor." (Abu Dawud, Al-Adab, 122-123.)

🤝 Love and Respect Among Friends
We should be careful when choosing friends; we should befriend people of good character and good manners. Our Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "A person follows the way of their friend. So let each of you consider carefully whom you befriend." (Abu Dawud, Al-Adab, 16.)

Hadith: "None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself." (Bukhari, Faith, 7.)

👩‍🏫 Love and Respect in Our Relationships with Teachers
Teachers hold a special importance because they train members of every other profession. They guide us toward what is good, beautiful, and right; they instill in us love of Allah, the Prophet (peace be upon him), religion, homeland, nation, and flag.

3️⃣ Getting to Know a Surah: Al-Fatihah and Its Meaning

Surah Al-Fatihah is the first surah of the Qur'an and was revealed while our Prophet (peace be upon him) was living in Mecca. It has seven verses. The word "Fatihah" literally means "to open, the beginning of something, the outset, the opening, the introduction."

Did you know? Surah Al-Fatihah is recited standing in every unit (rak'ah) of every prayer. A Muslim who performs the five daily prayers recites Surah Al-Fatihah forty times in a day. Our Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "There is no prayer for the one who does not recite Al-Fatihah." (Muslim, Salah, 111.)
