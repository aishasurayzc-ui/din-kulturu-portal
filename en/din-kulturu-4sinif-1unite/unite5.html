🧼 Unit 5 Topic Summary: Islam and Cleanliness

1️⃣ Islam and Cleanliness

Cleanliness is a person's purification from all visible and invisible impurity. Cleanliness is divided into two: physical cleanliness (keeping the body, clothes, and environment clean) and spiritual cleanliness (having a pure inner world and heart; having good character). In our culture, spiritual cleanliness is also called inner cleanliness and cleanliness of the heart.

Hadith: "A believer is one with whom others get along well. There is no good in one who does not get along well with others and cannot get along with others." (Ahmad ibn Hanbal, al-Musnad, Vol. 2, p. 400.)

Ablution
Purification

In Islamic culture, the term purification (taharah) is sometimes used instead of cleanliness. Allah loves those who are clean: "...Allah loves those who purify themselves." (Surah At-Tawbah, verse 108.) Our Prophet (pbuh): "Allah is beautiful and loves beauty; He is clean and loves cleanliness..." (Tirmidhi, Adab, 41.) — "...Cleanliness is half of faith." (Tirmidhi, Da'awat, 86.)

A Muslim about to pray must first perform ablution (wudu). Our Prophet (pbuh): "The key to Paradise is prayer, and the key to prayer is ablution." (Tirmidhi, Taharah, 1.) Those visiting the Kaaba must also be in a state of ablution.

Verse: "O you who believe! When you rise up for prayer, wash your faces and your hands up to the elbows, and wipe your heads, and wash your feet up to the ankles... Allah does not want to place a burden on you, but He wants to purify you and complete His favor upon you, that you may be grateful." (Surah Al-Ma'idah, verse 6.)

Steps of ablution: Make the intention and say the basmalah → Wash the hands → Rinse the mouth → Clean the nose → Wash the face → Wash the right, then the left arm up to the elbows → Wipe the head with a wet hand → Wipe the inside of the ears with the index/little finger and the back with the thumb → Wipe the neck → Wash the right, then the left foot up to the ankles.

🤔 Did You Know?

It takes about 400 years for a plastic bottle to decompose in nature — which is why recycling is both a religious and an environmental responsibility.

2️⃣ I Keep My Body and Clothes Clean

Physical cleanliness means keeping the hands, face, mouth, nose, feet, nails, hair — in short, the entire body — clean. Our hands are the organ that gets dirtiest; they should be washed before and after meals and after using the toilet.

Hadith: "The blessing of food lies in washing the hands before and after eating." (Abu Dawud, At'ima, 11.) — "Washing the hair and body by bathing once every seven days is a right that Allah has placed on every Muslim." (Bukhari, Jumu'ah, 12; Muslim, Jumu'ah, 9.)

Oral and dental health is important. The Prophet (pbuh) used to clean his teeth with a type of tree twig called the miswak. "Use the miswak... Gabriel recommended teeth-cleaning to me so persistently that I feared it would become obligatory for me and my community." (Ibn Majah, Taharah, 7.)

Care should be given to hair and nail cleanliness. Clothing cleanliness is also part of physical cleanliness: "Purify your garments." (Surah Al-Muddaththir, verse 4.) The saying "People are greeted according to their clothing and seen off according to their thinking" points to the importance of dressing cleanly.

❌ Common Misconceptions

❌ Some people think cleanliness means only physical cleanliness (washing).

✅ In Islam, cleanliness is a broad concept with both a physical (body, environment) and a spiritual dimension (honesty, integrity, purifying oneself from bad character).

🌍 A Real-Life Example

A student seeing litter in the schoolyard and throwing it in the trash bin is a fine example of both physical cleanliness and awareness of "the rights of others."

3️⃣ I Keep My Home and School Clean

Living in a clean and orderly home gives a person peace of mind. We should keep our room tidy, make our bed, and put our things in their proper place. We should also take responsibility for keeping shared spaces such as the bathroom, kitchen, and living room clean.

Keeping our school clean and orderly is important for our health and education. We must remember that littering the school means infringing on others' rights, that is, violating the rights of others.

4️⃣ I Keep My Environment Clean

The environment is the setting we live in, which constantly affects us (air, water, soil, plants, animals). Every year 743 million tons of our fertile topsoil is lost to erosion; worldwide, an area 3 times the size of Switzerland is lost to desertification every year.

Decomposition times in nature: Plastic bottle: 400 years, glass bottle: 4,000 years, plastic material: 1,000 years, paper/newspaper: 3 months.

Allah has created everything in due measure and balance: "Indeed, We have created all things in due measure and balance." (Surah Al-Qamar, verse 49.) "And the heaven He raised and set up the balance. So do not transgress the balance." (Surah Ar-Rahman, verses 7-8.)

The elements that make up the environment are a trust from Allah to humanity. "Corruption has appeared on land and sea because of what people's hands have earned..." (Surah Ar-Rum, verse 41.)

Our Prophet (pbuh) encouraged planting trees: "If a Muslim plants a tree or sows a crop, and a bird, a person, or an animal eats from it, it counts as charity for him." (Bukhari, Adab, 27.) "If any of you has a palm shoot in his hand and the Hour is about to come, let him plant it if he can!" (Ahmad ibn Hanbal.)

Hadith: "Beware of the three acts that bring curses: relieving yourselves in water sources, in the middle of paths, and in shaded resting places." (Abu Dawud, Taharah, 14.)

We should throw trash in the bin, separate recyclable waste into recycling bins, and protect forests and trees.
