🕌 Unit 4 Topic Summary: Getting to Know Prophet Muhammad

1️⃣ The Environment Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) Was Born Into

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was born on the Arabian Peninsula, in Mecca, within the borders of present-day Saudi Arabia. Mecca is described in the Qur'an as "the mother of cities."

Social Situation: Society was based on tribalism. A tribe is a community of people descended from the same ancestor, bound together by blood and lineage. Having a son was considered an honor, while daughters were not valued. Society included slaves who were granted no rights. Those who lived a settled life in the city/village were called hadari, while those who lived a nomadic life were called bedouin. The camel was an indispensable animal in desert conditions.

Verse: "And when one of them is given news of [the birth of] a female, his face becomes dark, and he suppresses grief." (Surah az-Zukhruf, verse 17.)

2️⃣ The Family of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)

  • Father: Abdullah — passed away in Medina before Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was born.
  • Mother: Amina — from the Banu Zuhrah tribe, she raised her son without a father.
  • Grandfather: Abdul-Muttalib — leader of Mecca, deeply loved his grandson.
  • Uncle: Abu Talib — the one who cared for Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) the most (his other uncles: Hamza, Abbas, Zubayr).
  • Foster mother: Halima — nursed him for four years (his first foster mother was Thuwaybah, who nursed him for a week).
The Event of the Elephant: Abraha, the governor of Yemen, marched on Mecca with elephants to destroy the Kaaba. Allah scattered his army with stones dropped by birds from the sky, and protected the Kaaba. This event is described in the Qur'an's Surah al-Fil (The Elephant).

3️⃣ His Birth, Childhood, and Youth

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was born on 20 April (12 Rabi' al-Awwal) 571 in Mecca, an orphan since his father had already died. The name "Muhammad" means "the praised one, one who possesses good qualities."

After staying with Halima for four years, he returned to his mother. At age six, his mother Amina passed away at Abwa while returning from Medina. At age eight, his grandfather Abdul-Muttalib passed away, and entrusted him to the care of his uncle Abu Talib. Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) lived with Abu Talib until the age of twenty-five.

In his youth he engaged in trade; his honesty and trustworthiness earned him the admiration of the people of Mecca, and he was given the title "al-Amin" (the Trustworthy). He married Khadijah (RA), for whom he had been conducting trade; they had six children (four daughters: Zaynab, Ruqayyah, Umm Kulthum, Fatimah; two sons: Qasim, Abdullah). He later had another son, Ibrahim, with Maria.

Arbitration Over the Kaaba: When a dispute broke out among the tribes over who would place the Black Stone (Hajar al-Aswad) back during the repair of the Kaaba, it was decided that the first person to arrive at the Kaaba would act as arbiter. That person was Prophet Muhammad (PBUH); he placed the stone on a cloth and had a representative of each tribe hold the cloth, finding a fair solution.

In his youth he also took part in the Hilf al-Fudul, an alliance formed to right the wrongs in the city.

4️⃣ The Years in Mecca and Medina

The First Revelation: When Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was 40 years old, the angel Gabriel appeared to him in the Cave of Hira and recited the first five verses of Surah al-Alaq. The First Muslims: Khadijah, Ali, Abu Bakr, and Zayd (RA).

Hardships: As the persecution by the idolaters increased, some Muslims migrated to Abyssinia in 615-616. Because of the deaths of Khadijah (RA) and Abu Talib in 620, this year is called the Year of Sorrow. This was followed by the miracle of the Isra (the night journey from Mecca to al-Masjid al-Aqsa in Jerusalem) and the Mi'raj (the ascension from al-Masjid al-Aqsa to the presence of Allah). Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) went to Ta'if to spread Islam but was received poorly.

The Pledges of Aqaba: In 620, 6 people from Medina (First Pledge of Aqaba); in 621, 75 people from Medina (Second Pledge of Aqaba) pledged allegiance to Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).

The Hijra: In 622, Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) left Ali (RA) in his bed and left Mecca together with Abu Bakr (RA); they hid in the Cave of Thawr, then reached Medina. In Medina, he had the Prophet's Mosque (Masjid al-Nabawi) built, established a bond of brotherhood between the Meccan Muhajirun and the Medinan Ansar, and signed the Constitution of Medina, which allowed different faiths to live together.

Wars and the Conquest: The Meccans launched the Battles of Badr, Uhud, and the Trench in 624, 625, and 627, but did not succeed. The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah was signed in 628; when the treaty was broken, the bloodless Conquest of Mecca took place in 630, and the idols in the Kaaba were removed.

The Farewell Pilgrimage and His Passing: In 632 he performed the Farewell Pilgrimage and delivered the Farewell Sermon (the inviolability of life/property/honor, the prohibition of usury and blood feuds, the protection of women's rights, the equality of people, and holding fast to the Qur'an and the Sunnah). He passed away in Medina on 8 June 632; his tomb is in the Rawdah al-Mutahharah, adjoining the Prophet's Mosque.

5️⃣ Getting to Know a Prayer: The Salli and Barik Prayers

The Salli and Barik prayers are recited in the final sitting of the prayer (salah) to send blessings (salawat) upon our Prophet (PBUH).

PrayerPronunciationMeaning
Salli PrayerAllâhümme salli alâ Muhammedin ve alâ âli Muhammedin kemâ salleyte alâ İbrâhîme ve alâ âli İbrâhîme inneke hamîdün mecîd.O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You sent blessings upon Abraham and the family of Abraham.
Barik PrayerAllâhümme bârik alâ Muhammedin ve alâ âli Muhammedin kemâ bârekte alâ İbrâhîme ve alâ âli İbrâhîme inneke hamîdün mecîd.O Allah, bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Abraham and the family of Abraham.
The family members, household, and close relatives of our Prophet (PBUH) are called the Ahl al-Bayt.
