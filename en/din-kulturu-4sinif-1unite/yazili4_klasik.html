UNIT 4: GETTING TO KNOW PROPHET MUHAMMAD & UNIT 5: ISLAM AND CLEANLINESS

A) Answer the following questions briefly.

1. Why did the people of Mecca give Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) the nickname "al-Amin"? (13 points)

2. In the hadith about the miswak, who strongly recommended tooth-cleaning to our Prophet? (13 points)

GRADE 4 TERM 2 EXAM 2 (CLASSIC) — ANSWER KEY
UNIT 4: GETTING TO KNOW PROPHET MUHAMMAD & UNIT 5: ISLAM AND CLEANLINESS

Knowledge Questions (1-4):

1. His honesty and trustworthiness.

2. Gabriel (as).

3. Surah Al-Muddaththir; it commands us to give importance to bodily/outward cleanliness.

4. Hijrah is migrating from Mecca to Medina; those who migrated are called Muhajirs, and the people of Medina who helped them are called Ansar.

Verse Interpretation Questions (5-7) — Assessment Criteria:

5. Interpretations that connect physical/spiritual cleanliness to Allah's love receive full points.

6. Interpretations that address bodily cleanliness being presented as a religious duty receive full points.

7. Interpretations that establish a cause-and-effect relationship between human behavior and environmental degradation receive full points.
