UNIT 1: RELIGIOUS EXPRESSIONS IN DAILY LIFE

A) Answer the following questions briefly.

1. Which religious expression do we use when someone helps us? State its meaning as well. (13 points)

2. What does the phrase "wa la ilaha ghayruk" in the Subhanaka prayer mean? (13 points)

3. In what situation, and with what meaning, is the phrase "Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un" said? (13 points)

4. Which expression do we use when we hope something will happen? (13 points)

B) Read the following verse translations and interpret the message given in your own words.

5. "All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds." (Surah Al-Fatihah, verse 2) What kind of feeling does this verse tell us we should approach Allah with? Interpret it. (16 points)

6. "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you (in favor)..." (Surah Ibrahim, verse 7) What does this verse tell us about the result of being grateful? Explain with an example from your daily life. (16 points)
