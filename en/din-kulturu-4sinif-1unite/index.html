📌 Religious Expressions in Everyday Conversation (1/9)

  • These expressions appear in almost every moment of daily life: when starting a task, receiving a blessing, meeting someone, or noticing a mistake we've made... Each one has its own occasion for use and its own spiritual meaning.

📌 Religious Expressions in Everyday Conversation (2/9)

  • 📿 Basmala — "Bismillahirrahmanirrahim"
    Meaning: "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful." It is said when starting a meal, a lesson, a task, or a journey. Tasks begun with the Basmala are believed to become blessed and good. This is why our Prophet advised us to begin every good deed with the Basmala.

📌 Religious Expressions in Everyday Conversation (3/9)

  • 🙏 Hamd (Praise) — "Alhamdulillah"
    Meaning: "All praise and thanks belong to Allah." It is said when we receive a blessing, good news, or success, in order to praise Allah and thank Him. Giving praise helps us appreciate the value of what we have.

📌 Religious Expressions in Everyday Conversation (4/9)

  • 💚 Gratitude — "Allah'a şükür" (Thanks be to Allah)
    Meaning: expressing gratitude for the blessings Allah has given us. Gratitude is not just something spoken aloud; it also means using a blessing properly and sharing it with others.

📌 Religious Expressions in Everyday Conversation (5/9)

  • 🤝 Greeting — "As-salamu alaykum" / "Wa alaykumu s-salam"
    Meaning: "May Allah's peace/safety be upon you." This is an expression of love and peace that Muslims say to one another when they meet. Our Prophet recommended spreading the greeting of peace as a practice that increases love among people.

📌 Religious Expressions in Everyday Conversation (6/9)

  • 🕌 Takbir — "Allahu Akbar"
    Meaning: "Allah is the Greatest." It is said to declare Allah's greatness and supremacy. It is an expression we hear often at the start of and during prayer, in the call to prayer, and during religious holidays.
  • 🌙 Salawat — "Allahumma salli ala sayyidina Muhammad"
    Meaning: to pray for our Prophet, offering him love and respect. Reciting salawat whenever the Prophet's name is mentioned shows our devotion to him.

📌 Religious Expressions in Everyday Conversation (7/9)

  • 😔 Seeking Forgiveness — "Astaghfirullah"
    Meaning: "I seek forgiveness from Allah." It is said when we accidentally say something wrong, make a mistake, or feel regret, to ask Allah's forgiveness. Seeking forgiveness reminds us that we must learn from our mistakes and try to correct ourselves.

📌 Religious Expressions in Everyday Conversation (8/9)

  • ✨ Glorification — "Subhanallah"
    Meaning: "I declare Allah free from all imperfection." It is said to express that Allah can have no flaw and to declare His majesty; it is also often said in the face of astonishing beauty.

📌 Religious Expressions in Everyday Conversation (9/9)

  • Did you know? When beginning to read the Qur'an, we say "A'udhu billahi mina'sh-shaytani'r-rajim" (I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan). When someone sneezes and says "Alhamdulillah," they are answered with "Yarhamukallah" (May Allah have mercy on you); that person then responds with "Yahdikumullah."

🤔 Did You Know?

Why do we say "Alhamdulillah" when we sneeze, and why do those around us say "Yarhamukallah"? This tradition of mutual prayer comes from the sunnah of our Prophet.

📌 Religious Expressions Used in Wishes and Prayers (1/3)

  • A prayer (dua) is when a servant turns to Allah to ask Him for something, to plead with Him, and to open their heart. When we make good wishes for our loved ones, we are really asking Allah for something on their behalf. That is why we use many "wish expressions" in our daily life:

📌 Religious Expressions Used in Wishes and Prayers (2/3)

  • May Allah grant healing — Said to someone who is ill, wishing them a swift recovery.
  • Go in Allah's safekeeping — Said to someone setting off on a journey, wishing that their path and themselves be under Allah's protection.
  • InshaAllah — Means "if Allah permits/wills"; it is said when speaking of something we hope will happen in the future. This phrase reminds us that everything happens by Allah's permission.
  • May Allah be pleased with you — Said to thank someone who has done us a kindness or helped us.
  • Amin — Means "So be it, accept it, O Allah." It is said together by everyone at the end of a prayer, wishing for that prayer to be accepted.
  • May Allah grant abundance — Said to wish someone abundance in their earnings, efforts, or home.
  • May it be good for you (Hayırlı olsun) — Said to someone moving into a new home, starting a new job, or acquiring something new.
  • May Allah make it easy — Said to wish strength and ease to someone undertaking a difficult task.
  • May Allah have mercy on them — Said when a deceased person is remembered, asking Allah's mercy for them.

📌 Religious Expressions Used in Wishes and Prayers (3/3)

  • Let's not forget: When we use these expressions, we are not just saying words; we are also sincerely praying for the person in front of us. That is why we should say these words from the heart.

❌ Common Misconceptions

❌ Some people think saying "InshaAllah" is a vague expression meaning "maybe it will happen, maybe it won't."

✅ In fact, "InshaAllah" expresses our belief that everything happens by Allah's permission, with a firm sense of trust in Him (tawakkul).

📌 Getting to Know a Prayer: The Subhanaka Prayer and Its Meaning (1/3)

  • 🕋 سُبْحَانَكَ اَللّٰهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ وَتَعَالٰى جَدُّكَ وَلاَ اِلٰهَ غَيْرُكَ — Subhanaka Allahumma wa bihamdika wa tabaraka ismuka wa ta'ala jadduka wa la ilaha ghayruk.

📌 Getting to Know a Prayer: The Subhanaka Prayer and Its Meaning: Word-by-word meaning

  • Subhanaka Allahumma wa bihamdika — "O Allah! You are free of all imperfection, I always glorify and praise You as such."
  • wa tabaraka ismuka — "Your name is blessed."
  • wa ta'ala jadduka — "Your being and majesty are above all things."
  • wa la ilaha ghayruk — "There is no god but You."

📌 Getting to Know a Prayer: The Subhanaka Prayer and Its Meaning (3/3)

  • When is it recited? The Subhanaka prayer is recited right after standing for prayer and saying the opening (iftitah) takbir, before beginning to recite the Qur'an. That is why this prayer can also be called "the key to the prayer (salah)."
  • Why is it important? With the Subhanaka prayer, we begin our salah by praising and exalting Allah and remembering that there is no god but Him. This prayer prepares our hearts for worship and renews our devotion to Allah.

🌍 A Real-Life Example

When a friend gets a high grade on an exam and says "Alhamdulillah, I'm so happy!", it shows both that they appreciate the value of their success and that they are celebrating it by thanking Allah.

📌 Expanded Glossary of Concepts (1/7)

  • The basic concepts we need to know to better understand religious expressions are grouped by topic:

📌 Expanded Glossary of Concepts: Concepts About Allah and Our Prophet

  • Allah — The one and only Creator of the universe and of us; He has no spouse, likeness, or partner.
  • Prophet — A messenger sent by Allah to show people the right path.
  • Revelation (Wahy) — The knowledge and message Allah sends to His prophets.
  • Angel — A being created by Allah from light, which never sins and constantly obeys Him.

📌 Expanded Glossary of Concepts: What Allah Has Given Us

  • Blessing (Nimet) — Goodness and beauty given by Allah to His servants (such as health, family, food).
  • Mercy (Rahmah) — Allah's endless compassion and mercy toward His servants.
  • Grace/Favor — Goodness and generosity Allah grants us even though we do not deserve it.

📌 Expanded Glossary of Concepts: The Reward for Our Actions

  • Sevap (Reward) — The reward, in Allah's sight, for a good deed.
  • Sin (Günah) — A bad deed that Allah does not approve of and has forbidden.
  • Halal — Something our religion permits us to do.
  • Haram — Something our religion strictly forbids.

📌 Expanded Glossary of Concepts: Concepts About the Afterlife

  • Paradise (Jannah) — The beautiful, flawless place that people who do good, beautiful deeds will go to after death.
  • Hell (Jahannam) — The place full of torment said to await those who do evil and do not repent.

📌 Expanded Glossary of Concepts: Concepts About Acts of Worship

  • Worship (Ibadah) — All the actions we perform to show our love, respect, and devotion to Allah.
  • Salah (Prayer) — The most important physical act of worship, performed by Muslims five times a day.
  • Adhan (Call to Prayer) — The call recited from minarets announcing that the time for prayer has come.
  • Sawm (Fasting) — The act of worship of abstaining from eating, drinking, and bodily desires from dawn until the evening call to prayer.
  • Zakat — Muslims who have reached a certain level of wealth giving a portion of their possessions to those in need.
  • Sacrifice (Qurban) — An animal slaughtered under specific conditions in order to earn Allah's approval.
  • Hajj — The pilgrimage to Mecca that able Muslims undertake once in their lifetime.

📌 Expanded Glossary of Concepts: Blessed Times and Days

  • Ramadan — The blessed month of fasting, during which the Qur'an began to be revealed.
  • Bayram (Religious Holiday) — Special days celebrated in our religion with joy and brotherhood (such as Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha).

📋 Unit 1 Topic Summary: Religious Expressions in Daily Life

What are religious expressions? People express their thoughts and feelings through language. In our language, there are many words and concepts that emerged through the influence of our religion and have become part of our daily conversation. These are called religious expressions. We use these expressions when we notice our joy, our sorrow, our gratitude, our wish, or a mistake we have made. Using these expressions appropriately and consciously helps us remember Allah, our Prophet, and the good character our religion teaches.

2️⃣ Dilek ve Dualarda Geçen Dinî İfadeler

Dua, kulun Allah'a yönelerek O'ndan bir şey istemesi, O'na yalvarması ve gönlünü açmasıdır. Sevdiklerimize iyi dileklerde bulunurken de aslında onlar için Allah'tan bir şeyler dileriz. İşte bu yüzden günlük hayatımızda birçok "dilek ifadesi" kullanırız:

  • Allah şifa versin — Hasta olan birine, acil bir iyileşme dileğiyle söylenir.
  • Allah'a emanet ol — Yolculuğa çıkan birinin yolunun ve kendisinin Allah'ın korumasında olmasını dilemek için söylenir.
  • İnşallah — "Allah izin verirse/dilerse" anlamına gelir; gelecekte olmasını umduğumuz bir işten bahsederken söylenir. Bu söz, her şeyin Allah'ın izniyle gerçekleştiğini hatırlamamızı sağlar.
  • Allah razı olsun — Bize iyilik yapan, yardım eden birine teşekkür etmek için söylenir.
  • Amin — "Öyle olsun, kabul et Allah'ım" anlamındadır. Bir dua bittiğinde, o duanın kabul olması dileğiyle hep birlikte söylenir.
  • Allah bereket versin — Birinin kazancına, emeğine veya evine bolluk dilemek için söylenir.
  • Hayırlı olsun — Yeni bir eve taşınan, yeni bir işe başlayan ya da yeni bir eşyaya kavuşan birine söylenir.
  • Allah kolaylık versin — Zor bir işe girişen birine güç ve kolaylık dilemek için söylenir.
  • Allah rahmet eylesin — Vefat etmiş bir kimse anıldığında, onun için Allah'tan merhamet dilenerek söylenir.
Unutmayalım: Bu ifadeleri kullanırken sadece bir söz söylemiş olmuyoruz; aynı zamanda karşımızdaki kişi için içtenlikle dua etmiş oluyoruz. Bu yüzden bu sözleri gönülden söylemeliyiz.

❌ Yanlış Bilinenler

❌ Bazıları "İnşallah" demeyi "belki olur belki olmaz" gibi bir belirsizlik ifadesi sanır.

✅ Aslında "İnşallah", her şeyin Allah'ın izniyle gerçekleştiğine olan inancımızı, kesin bir tevekkül bilinciyle ifade eder.

3️⃣ Bir Dua Tanıyorum: Sübhâneke Duası ve Anlamı

سُبْحَانَكَ اَللّٰهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ وَتَعَالٰى جَدُّكَ وَلاَ اِلٰهَ غَيْرُكَ

Sübhânekellâhümme ve bi hamdik ve tebârekesmük ve teâlâ ceddük ve lâ ilâhe ğayrük.

Kelime kelime anlamı:

  • Sübhânekellâhümme ve bi hamdik — "Allah'ım! Sen eksik sıfatlardan uzaksın, seni daima böyle tenzih eder ve överim."
  • ve tebârekesmük — "Senin adın mübarektir."
  • ve teâlâ ceddük — "Varlığın, şanın her şeyden üstündür."
  • ve lâ ilâhe ğayrük — "Senden başka ilah yoktur."

Ne zaman okunur? Sübhâneke duası, namaza durup iftitah (başlangıç) tekbiri aldıktan hemen sonra, Kur'an okumaya başlamadan önce okunur. Bu yüzden bu duaya "namazın anahtarı" da denilebilir.

Neden önemlidir? Sübhâneke duasıyla namaza, Allah'ı övüp yücelterek ve O'ndan başka ilah olmadığını hatırlayarak başlarız. Bu dua, kalbimizi ibadete hazırlar ve Allah'a olan bağlılığımızı tazeler.

🌍 Gerçek Hayattan Bir Örnek

Bir arkadaşın sınavdan yüksek not aldığında "Elhamdülillah, çok sevindim!" demesi, hem başarısının kıymetini bilmesini hem de bunu Allah'a şükrederek kutlamasını gösterir.

📖 Genişletilmiş Kavramlar Sözlüğü

Dinî ifadeleri daha iyi anlayabilmek için bilmemiz gereken temel kavramlar, konularına göre gruplandırılmıştır:

Allah ve Peygamberimizle İlgili Kavramlar

  • Allah — Evrenin ve bizim tek yaratıcımız; eşi, benzeri ve ortağı yoktur.
  • Peygamber — Allah'ın, insanlara doğru yolu göstermek için gönderdiği elçi.
  • Vahiy — Allah'ın, peygamberlerine gönderdiği bilgi ve mesaj.
  • Melek — Allah'ın nurdan yarattığı, hiç günah işlemeyen, sürekli O'na itaat eden varlık.

Allah'ın Bize Verdikleri

  • Nimet — Allah'ın kullarına verdiği güzellik ve iyilik (sağlık, aile, yiyecek gibi).
  • Rahmet — Allah'ın kullarına olan sonsuz merhameti ve acıması.
  • Lütuf — Allah'ın, hak etmediğimiz hâlde bize bahşettiği iyilik ve ihsan.

Davranışlarımızın Karşılığı

  • Sevap — İyi bir davranışın Allah katındaki karşılığı, mükâfatı.
  • Günah — Allah'ın hoş görmediği, yasakladığı kötü davranış.
  • Helal — Dinimizin yapılmasına izin verdiği şey.
  • Haram — Dinimizin kesin olarak yasakladığı şey.

Ahiretle İlgili Kavramlar

  • Cennet — İyi, güzel işler yapan insanların öldükten sonra gideceği, hiç eksiği olmayan güzel yer.
  • Cehennem — Kötülük yapıp tövbe etmeyenlerin gideceği söylenen, azap dolu yer.

İbadetlerle İlgili Kavramlar

  • İbadet — Allah'a olan sevgi, saygı ve bağlılığımızı göstermek için yaptığımız davranışların tümü.
  • Namaz — Müslümanların günde beş vakit kıldığı, bedenle yapılan en önemli ibadet.
  • Ezan — Namaz vaktinin geldiğini bildiren, minarelerden okunan çağrı.
  • Oruç — İmsak vaktinden akşam ezanına kadar yeme, içme ve nefsin isteklerinden uzak durma ibadeti.
  • Zekat — Belli bir zenginliğe ulaşan Müslümanların, mallarının bir kısmını ihtiyaç sahiplerine vermesi.
  • Kurban — Allah'ın rızasını kazanmak amacıyla belirli şartlarda kesilen hayvan.
  • Hac — Gücü yeten Müslümanların, ömürlerinde bir kez Mekke'ye yaptığı ibadet yolculuğu.

Mübarek Zaman ve Günler

  • Ramazan — Oruç tutulan, Kur'an'ın indirilmeye başladığı mübarek ay.
  • Bayram — Dinimizde sevinç ve kardeşlik duygularıyla kutlanan özel günler (Ramazan ve Kurban Bayramı gibi).
