auto_fix_high Content Creation Guide (For Teachers) expand_more

Ready-made AI prompt templates you can use when preparing a new unit or activity. Just fill in the bracketed fields ([Subject Name], [Grade Level], [Topic Name], etc.) and paste it into an AI tool.

description Unit Summary Text

Prepare a unit summary text for the subject and topic specified below, appropriate for the grade level and aligned with the Turkish Century Maarif Model.

* Subject: [Subject Name, e.g. Religious Culture and Ethics]
* Grade Level: [Grade, e.g. Grade 7]
* Topic / Unit: [Topic Name]

Content Requirements:
1. Use plain, fluent language that students can easily understand.
2. List key concepts as bullet points with bold text.
3. Add 3 'Think-Share' questions at the end of the topic for students to test themselves.
4. Create 1 highlighted summary/spotlight sentence for use in the page design.

quiz Interactive Questions and Activities

Generate interactive question and activity content for the specified subject topic, to be used on the web portal.

* Subject & Grade: [e.g. Grade 6 Religious Culture]
* Topic: [Topic Name]

Content Format:
1. Multiple Choice: 5 questions with 4 options each (indicate the correct answer and a brief explanation).
2. True / False: 5 True/False statements that assess key points of the topic.
3. Matching: 5 concepts with their corresponding definitions/explanations.
4. Add the answers to all questions at the bottom of the page as an 'Answer Key'.

style Summary Card Content

Prepare short and impactful content to be displayed on the summary cards (Card UI) in the course material portal.

* Topic: [Topic Name]

Output Format:
1. Card Title: An attention-grabbing title of maximum 5 words.
2. Short Description: A spotlight text of maximum 20-25 words that captures the essence of the topic.
3. Keywords / Tags: 3 tags related to the topic (e.g. #Concept1, #Concept2).
4. Visual Suggestion: An icon or image idea symbolizing the topic, for use at the top of the card.

edit_document Exam Preparation Worksheet

Create an 'Exam Preparation Worksheet' appropriate for the grade level and curriculum.

* Subject: [Subject Name]
* Grade Level: [Grade]
* Term/Exam: [e.g. 1st Term, 2nd Exam]

Content Structure:
1. Section A: 5 Fill-in-the-Blank questions.
2. Section B: 5 Definition/Concept explanation questions.
3. Section C: 3 Open-Ended (interpretation and inference) questions.
4. Add a teacher assessment and scoring key template at the bottom.
