🌌 Unit 1 Topic Summary: Belief in Allah

1️⃣ The Perfect Order in the Universe

The universe is the totality of beings that includes the world we live on along with all the stars and planets; this is also called the cosmos. Human beings are distinguished from other living things by their capacity for reason; they observe, think about, and question their surroundings.

The balanced and proportionate structure of the human body contains clues to the order in the universe. Organs such as the eyes, ears, and nose were created according to human needs. Snow that falls in winter protects the soil and increases groundwater; the rising heat of summer allows crops to ripen. None of this is a coincidence; everything happens within a perfect order.

🤔 Did You Know?

Al-Asma al-Husna are Allah's 99 beautiful names; each one describes a different attribute of His.

2️⃣ The Existence and Oneness of Allah

No being can come into existence on its own or by chance. Just as every book has an author and every painting has a painter, the perfect order in the universe also has a Creator: Allah (swt).

Verse: "He who created and proportioned, and who determined and guided." — "He is the Originator of the heavens and the earth without any precedent. When He decrees a matter, He only says to it, 'Be,' and it is."

Tawhid

Believing that Allah (swt) is one and unique, that He has no equal or likeness, and worshipping Him alone without associating anything with Him is called tawhid. Tawhid is one of the fundamental pillars of belief in Islam and is the essence of the religion. Believing in Allah while also accepting other beings as deities is called shirk.

Verse: "Had there been within the heavens and earth gods besides Allah, they both would have been ruined..." (Surah Al-Anbiya, verse 22) — "Indeed, all things We created with predestined measure." (Surah Al-Qamar, verse 49)

❌ Common Misconceptions

✅ The flawless order in the universe (the orbits of the planets, the harmony of living things) also points to Allah's existence through reason.

🌍 A Real-Life Example

A flower sprouting from a seed and growing in a set order is a reflection, observable in daily life, of Allah's names Al-Alim (the All-Knowing) and Al-Qadir (the All-Powerful).

3️⃣ Allah's Beautiful Names (Al-Asma al-Husna)

Al-Asma al-Husna means "the most beautiful names" in Arabic; these are the names that describe Allah's (swt) power, perfection, and uniqueness.

  • Ar-Rahman — The One who shows compassion to all created beings, whose mercy is infinite.
  • Ar-Rahim — The One who grants unearned blessings to His creation, and who will show mercy to and reward His believing servants in the hereafter.
  • Al-Alim — The One who knows everything, hidden or open; whose knowledge is infinite.
  • As-Sami — The All-Hearing.
  • Al-Basir — The All-Seeing.
  • Al-Qadir — The One who has power over all things; whose power is infinite.
Hadith: Our Prophet (pbuh), giving the example of a mother anxiously searching for her lost child and embracing her when she finds him, said: "Allah's mercy toward His servants is far greater than this mother's mercy toward her child."

The names Ar-Rahman and Ar-Rahim appear together in the phrase "Bismillahirrahmanirrahim" (the basmala), which we use often in daily life.

4️⃣ Learning a Surah: Surah Al-Ikhlas

Surah Al-Ikhlas is one of the short chapters of the Holy Qur'an and has 4 verses. The word "Ikhlas" means "sincerity, truthfulness, purity, and cleanliness" in the dictionary. The surah includes Allah's (swt) beautiful names Ahad (the One and Only) and Samad (the One who needs nothing).

RecitationMeaning
