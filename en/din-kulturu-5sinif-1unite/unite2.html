🕌 Unit 2 Topic Summary: Being in the Presence of Allah: The Worship of Prayer

1️⃣ The Worship of Prayer

Prayer (Salah) is a physical act of worship that begins with the takbir and ends with the taslim (salam), consisting of specific movements and words. Prayer is the most important pillar of Islam after the declaration of faith (kalima-i shahadah). It is obligatory (fard) for every mentally sound Muslim who has reached puberty to perform the five daily prayers.

Verse: "...Establish prayer, for prayer has been decreed upon the believers at specific times." — Hadith: "The key to Paradise is prayer, and the key to prayer is ablution."

Types of Prayer:

  • Obligatory (fard) prayers — Dawn, noon, afternoon, evening, night (5 times a day) + Friday prayer + Funeral prayer.
  • Necessary (wajib) prayers — Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha prayers, witr prayer.
  • Voluntary (nafil/sunnah) prayers — Those performed before/after obligatory prayers, tarawih, tahajjud.

🤔 Did You Know?

A prayer performed in congregation is 27 times more meritorious than a prayer performed alone.

2️⃣ How Prayer Is Performed

The Preconditions of Prayer (6): Purification from ritual impurity (performing ablution/ghusl), purification from physical filth (cleanliness of body/clothing/place), covering the body appropriately (satr al-awrah), the prayer time, facing the qibla, and intention.

Ablution steps: Intention → Hands → Mouth (3 times) → Nose (3 times) → Face (3 times) → Right-left arms to the elbows → Wiping the head → Wiping inside/behind the ears → Wiping the neck → Right-left feet to the ankles.

Tayammum: Dry ablution performed with clean earth/sand when water is unavailable or cannot be used. Ghusl: Washing the entire body so that no part remains dry (full ritual bath).

The Pillars of Prayer (6): The opening takbir (beginning with "Allahu Akbar"), qiyam (standing), qira'ah (reciting the Qur'an), ruku (bowing), sujud (prostration), and the final sitting (sitting long enough to recite the Tahiyyat).

A rak'ah is each unit of prayer consisting of standing, recitation, bowing, and two prostrations. In ruku, "Subhana Rabbiyal-Azim" is said 3 times; in sujud, "Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la" is said 3 times.

Congregational Prayer: Friday and Eid prayers must be performed in congregation at the mosque. Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) said: "A prayer performed in congregation is twenty-seven times more meritorious than a prayer performed alone." During the Friday prayer, the imam ascends the minbar (pulpit) and delivers the khutbah (sermon). The funeral prayer is performed standing only, without ruku or sujud.

❌ Common Misconceptions

❌ Some see prayer merely as an "obligation."

✅ According to the Qur'an, prayer is an act of worship that keeps a person away from indecency and evil, and brings them discipline and peace.

3️⃣ What the Worship of Prayer Brings to a Person

Prayer keeps a person's awareness of servitude to Allah alive and keeps them away from wrongdoing. Through ablution, a person is purified both spiritually and physically. The Holy Qur'an states: "...prayer restrains from indecency and evil..." According to our Prophet, sujud (prostration) is the moment when a servant is closest to Allah.

Among the Muslims who gather at mosques, a sense of brotherhood develops without distinction of race, color, or language; cooperation and solidarity increase.

4️⃣ Learning a Prayer: The Tahiyyat

Tahiyyat means being free from every shortcoming, greeting, and wishing health and well-being. This prayer, recited while sitting during prayer, begins with greetings and praise to Allah, continues with a request for mercy upon our Prophet, and ends with the declaration of faith (kalima-i shahadah).

RecitationMeaning

Esselamu aleyke eyyuhennebiyyu ve rahmetullahi ve berekatuhO Prophet! May Allah's mercy, blessing, and peace be upon you.
